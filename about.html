About DPL

DPL is the private scorekeeping app of the Duggleby Poker League, a group of friends who play poker together. It runs on the league's own small computer at the table: it keeps the tournament clock, records results and keeps the league standings.

DPL app Backups is the part of DPL that copies the league's own backup files into the league administrator's Google Drive, so the records survive if the computer fails.

The league's stats are on this site, locked with a password shared among league members.
