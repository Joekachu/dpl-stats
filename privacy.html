Privacy policy

DPL app Backups · Duggleby Poker League

What the app does with Google

DPL app Backups copies the Duggleby Poker League's own backup files (the league's records: players, games, results and standings) from the league's computer into a folder called DPL Backups in the Google Drive of the league administrator who connected it.

What it can see

It asks Google only for permission to manage the files it creates itself (drive.file). It cannot see, read or change any other file in that Google Drive, and it does not use the account's email, contacts or any other Google data.

What it keeps and shares

  • The league's computer keeps only the permission Google grants for those uploads. It never stores a Google password.
  • No data is sold, shared with anyone else or used for advertising.
  • The backups contain only the league's own poker records.

Stopping

The administrator can disconnect Google Drive in the app at any time, or remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already uploaded stay in their Drive until they delete them.

Questions

Contact the league administrator.
